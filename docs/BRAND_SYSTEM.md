# Open Claude — Brand system

Version 1.0 · 18 October 2026

## Purpose
Make AI useful and accessible, turning ideas into real work with context, control and transparency.

## Archetype
Sage + Creator. Explain uncertainty clearly, then help users turn ideas into artifacts and software.

## Positioning
An extensible AI workspace connecting Chat, Code and Cowork. Never claim a tool ran unless there is a verifiable result.

## Voice
Human, calm, intelligent, direct and curious. Explain what happened, what was preserved and how to retry. Use Brazilian Portuguese by default.

## Visual system
Warm editorial surfaces inspired by the calm feel of conversational AI, with original geometry and tokens rather than copying another company's logo.

- Charcoal: `#171513` background
- Surface: `#211E1B`
- Raised surface: `#292521`
- Paper: `#F5F0E8` main text and primary button
- Secondary text: `#B8AEA3`
- Terracotta: `#E98A68` brand and focus
- Peach: `#F3B69C` soft highlights
- Border: `#3A332E`
- Success: `#8FC5A4`
- Error: `#F19A92`

## Typography
Space Grotesk for titles and brand; DM Sans for interface; system monospace for code. Use clear hierarchy and comfortable line height.

## Symbol
A four-point geometric glint plus a smaller spark represents focus, discovery and multiple tasks converging. Keep clear space around the mark; do not stretch, tilt, add heavy glow, or imply Anthropic endorsement. Use at least 20 px for the symbol.

## UI rules
- Primary buttons: paper background and charcoal text, 12–14 px radius.
- Secondary buttons: raised surface and quiet border.
- Focus: terracotta outline and low-opacity halo.
- Cards: solid surfaces and thin borders; reserve blur for overlays.
- Errors always include text, not color alone.
- Empty states suggest a useful first action.
- Loading text should be contextual; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Use consistent tokens across marketing, auth, workspace, settings and docs.
- Never add a watermark to user content without an explicit choice.

## Naming and legal note
“Open Claude” may cause brand confusion or imply affiliation. This document is not a trademark search or legal review. Before commercial launch, assess a distinctive name, domain, handles and trademark risk.
